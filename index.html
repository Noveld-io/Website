The Platform

Analysis as infrastructure.

Noveld builds the analytical frameworks institutional decisions depend on: how a credit behaves under stress, what a recovery is worth, how sectoral and macroeconomic conditions bear on a valuation — answered through econometric models built and signed by professional economists.

What we sell is not a data feed or a dashboard. It is an owned, end-to-end analytical workflow embedded in the client's own process: the system ingests the relevant inputs, applies our economists' methodology, and produces a documented, signed deliverable — grounded in economic theory and the empirical record, traceable to its sources and its method.

Services

One methodology. Three applications.

Market & Credit Intelligence

Continuous, institutional-grade analysis of the markets and regions our clients are exposed to — macroeconomic and sectoral conditions, credit dynamics, and risk assessment, delivered through the platform.

Contracted Economic Analysis

Defined-scope engagements for investors, allocators, and the auditors and advisors who serve them: independent economic analysis produced under our documented methodology and signed by our economists.

Transaction & Capital-Structure Economics

The same analytical discipline applied to transactions: valuation economics, financing-feasibility assessment, and capital-structure design analysis. We evaluate structures conventional templates miss — combining asset-based, vendor, and structured layers on paper before anyone commits capital.

Approach

Rigor is the product.

Noveld is built on a simple premise: institutional decisions deserve analysis that is independent, methodical, and accountable. We are economists first — our work begins with theory and the empirical record, not with a narrative to justify.

AI makes our methodology scale; it does not replace judgment. Every analysis the platform produces is reviewed against our quantitative quality standards before a professional economist signs it. That signature is the deliverable.
